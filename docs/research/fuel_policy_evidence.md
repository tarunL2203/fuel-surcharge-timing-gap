## Decision

Use fuel-policy explainability and scenario comparison as the main use case. Mid-market shipper pricing/finance is the proposed initial audience, inferred from R2. Broker comparisons remain possible when both actual policies are supplied. No reliable prevalence estimate for the original weekly-carrier/monthly-or-quarterly-shipper pairing has been established by this review.

## Evidence labels

- DOCUMENTED_POLICY: exact identified policy version, scope, effective dates and calculation terms checked against a source. A source about general practice alone does not qualify a numeric policy.
- ILLUSTRATIVE: explicit project assumption, including all three initial policy seeds and their formula parameters.
- SYNTHETIC: artificial prices, shipments or invoices used for testing.
- HISTORICAL_SIMULATION: configured rules evaluated on historical real prices; no implication those rules were in force then.

Record price provenance and policy provenance separately. A hypothetical rule does not become DOCUMENTED_POLICY because its prices are real. Current published policies applied to earlier periods are simulations, not actual historical charges.

## Next business-validation evidence

Interview pricing/finance practitioners about index choice, review frequency, effective dates, holidays, rounding, caps/floors, mileage basis and all-in pricing. With permission, inspect anonymized terms and reconcile a small shipment/invoice sample. Record exceptions and reviewer sign-off. A small sample establishes applicability, not industry prevalence.

Do not claim low digital literacy from digital-adoption surveys. This project's case rests on explicit rule management, fragmented inputs and auditability; no adoption percentage is needed to justify the build.
